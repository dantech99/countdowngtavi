**Short answer:** on PS5, GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight on Thursday, November 19,
2026, local time in each country**. It is not a simultaneous worldwide launch:
it rolls out in waves, starting in Oceania and ending in the Americas.

If you just want to know how long is left, the [countdown on the home page](/en/)
is already set to your local time.

## What matters is your account's region, not where you are

The PlayStation Store uses **a single time per country**: the one for your PSN
account's region. It ignores your console's time zone and where you live. That
has two consequences almost nobody explains:

**1. In some parts of a country it unlocks before or after midnight.** The store
takes the country's reference time and applies it to the whole territory:

- **Mexico:** the reference is Central Time. In Quintana Roo (Cancún) it unlocks
  at 1:00 a.m. on the 19th. In Sonora, Sinaloa, Nayarit, Baja California Sur and
  Ciudad Juárez, at 11:00 p.m. on Wednesday 18. In Baja California (Tijuana,
  Mexicali), at 10:00 p.m. on the 18th.
- **Spain:** in the Canary Islands it unlocks at 23:00 on Wednesday 18.
- **United States:** the reference is Eastern Time. In Central Time it unlocks at
  11:00 p.m. on the 18th; in Mountain Time, at 10:00 p.m.; and in Pacific Time,
  at 9:00 p.m. on Wednesday 18.
- **Ecuador:** in the Galápagos it unlocks at 11:00 p.m. on the 18th.
- **Chile:** on Easter Island it unlocks at 10:00 p.m. on the 18th.

**2. If your account belongs to another country, that country's times apply.**
Venezuela and the Dominican Republic don't have their own PlayStation Store: if
you open the store from there, it redirects you to the United States one. With a
US account, GTA 6 unlocks at 05:00 UTC, which in Venezuela and the Dominican
Republic is **1:00 a.m. on Thursday 19**.

## What about the New Zealand trick?

New Zealand is the first country in the world to get the game: its store unlocks
it at 11:00 UTC on Wednesday 18, which is **6:00 a.m. on Wednesday 18 in
Colombia** and 5:00 a.m. in Mexico. That's an 18-hour head start.

But changing your console's region isn't enough. PlayStation doesn't let you
change the country of an existing PSN account, so you would need a new New
Zealand account and to buy the game there, in New Zealand dollars. For most
people it isn't worth it.

## What is confirmed and what isn't

**Confirmed by Rockstar:**

- The game launches on **November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Pre-load** opens on **November 12, 2026** for digital pre-orders.
- The physical edition comes with a **download code** inside the box, not a
  disc, and goes on sale on November 12 to support pre-loading.

**Verified by us on the PlayStation Store:**

- The release time for every country in the table, as it appears on the game's
  official listing in that country's store, checked on October 30, 2026.

**Not confirmed:**

- **Rockstar hasn't announced an official unlock time.** The times in the table
  are the ones Sony's store lists, and Sony or Rockstar could change them before
  launch. If that happens, we'll update this table.
- **Xbox:** we didn't check the Microsoft Store times. This table applies to PS5
  only.
- **Physical editions:** since they come with a code, they should in principle
  follow the same schedule as the digital version, but the time each retail
  store hands out the boxes depends on that store.

## How to be ready at midnight

1. Pre-order the digital version (or buy the physical one with a code) before
   November 12.
2. Pre-load the game from November 12, so you don't depend on your connection on
   launch night.
3. Leave your console on, or in rest mode with an internet connection.
4. Follow the countdown on the [countdown page](/en/).
